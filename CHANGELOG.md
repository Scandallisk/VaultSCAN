# VaultSCAN Forever 1.0.0

First Forever edition of VaultSCAN.

- Track current-character money and combined recorded-character money without bank dependencies.
- Show gold, silver and copper throughout the roster, totals and historical tooltips.
- Retain characters sharing a first name using unique GUIDs and display their surnames.
- Preserve ambiguous prototype records in a local archive excluded from totals.
- Keep Retail and Forever SavedVariables separate.
- Retain the familiar character overview, item-level history, played time, minimap launcher and daily money graph.
- Tighten column spacing and allocate spare width to Realm.
- Handle missing or failing item-level APIs without blocking character money recording.

For Forever 1.60.1 / interface 16001. Offline balances are last recorded observations. Log into every character you want tracked.
